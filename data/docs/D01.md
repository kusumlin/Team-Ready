Access to the Analytics Workspace (dashboards, saved reports and the Looker explore views) is granted through the Analytics Workspace Access form at go/analytics-access.
Every request needs approval from your direct manager before Data Platform can process it.
In the form, name the project or reporting task that needs access. Requests without a business reason are returned.
Data Platform processes approved requests within one business day.
Standard access is read-only. Editor access to shared dashboards needs a second approval from the dashboard owner.
