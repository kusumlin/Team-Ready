Use the Lumen VPN client from Self Service to reach internal tools from outside the office.
The VPN uses your Lumen account and multi-factor authentication.
If the VPN fails to connect, restart the client and confirm your device enrollment is complete.
Persistent VPN problems go to IT through go/it-help.
