Complete these steps in order during your first two weeks.
1. Finish laptop and account setup with IT on your first day.
2. Turn on multi-factor authentication for your Lumen account.
3. Complete Security Awareness Training within 14 days of your start date.
4. Join the Slack channels listed in the Slack Channels guide.
5. Request analytics tools after core account setup is complete.
6. Schedule your first 1:1 with your manager in your first week.
If a step is blocked for more than two days, tell your manager or post in #ask-people-ops.
