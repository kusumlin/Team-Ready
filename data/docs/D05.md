Submit expense reports in Expense Hub within 30 days of the purchase.
Select Client Meals as the category for meals with customers or partners.
Upload a photo or PDF of the itemized receipt. Credit card slips are not accepted.
Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll after your manager approves the report.
