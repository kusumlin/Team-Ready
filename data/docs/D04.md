Client meals require an itemized receipt, attendee names, and a business purpose.
The meal limit is 75 USD per person including tax and tip.
Alcohol is not reimbursable unless a director approves it in advance.
All expenses need manager approval in Expense Hub.
Book flights through the Lumen travel portal at least 14 days before travel when possible.
