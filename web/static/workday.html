              How TeamReady Works

              View live agent activity →
              What it's doing
              Every step by every agent is logged live on Agent Activity, including what each check stopped.
              Why it says what it says
              Each statement quotes an approved doc word for word, with the doc's date and owner. Anything it can't quote is removed.
              What data it uses
              Your name, role, team and office, and your question. Never private messages, reviews or pay.
              Before it acts
              It shows the system, the exact fields and the source doc. Nothing happens until you approve.
              Where people decide
              You approve, edit or reject every message and task. Access also needs your manager.
              What it won't do
              Touch passwords or MFA, approve its own requests, or decide pay, performance, benefits or anyone's job.
              When a person steps in
              Questions about pay, performance or HR stop before any search, and it names who to talk to.
              When it isn't sure
              Under 60/100 confidence, or when docs disagree, it doesn't guess. It finds who to ask and drafts the message.
              How to correct it
              Say "this looks wrong" to ask the doc's owner, or "wrong person" and routing learns.

              Prototype for the Workday "Build the Future of Work with AI" challenge. Lumen Labs and its people are fictional. Not a Workday product.
