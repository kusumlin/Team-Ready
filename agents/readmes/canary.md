**Canary New Hires: your onboarding docs get tested before your people do.**

Three synthetic new hires (a Data Analyst, a Backend Engineer, a Product Designer in the EU) walk the onboarding
checklist and their role's tasks, step by step. Every step is a real question to `teamready-knowledge`, through the same
verified-answer pipeline a new hire gets. Where a canary can't do a step from the docs, that's a wall:

- **Detour**: the checklist says what, not how, and doesn't link the doc that does
- **Stale-doc trap**: an out-of-date doc still comes up and gives different instructions
- **Circular**: the only source is the checklist itself
- **Dead end**: no doc covers it for that office

Each wall is checked against the Friction Map (did real new hires already hit it?) and gets a fix for the doc owner:
a one-line edit (drafted by the CREAO Doc Steward), an archive note, or a request to the right person. A person
approves before anything is sent.

Say **run**, **report**, **fixes**, or **approve F1**. Fictional people and synthetic docs only.
