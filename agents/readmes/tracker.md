# TeamReady Tracker: follow-ups so nothing stalls

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Watches the messages, 1:1 invites and access requests TeamReady sent for a new hire. When one gets no answer in time, it messages the new hire with a drafted, friendly follow-up. The draft passes the Guardian check and is sent only after the new hire approves.

**Say:** my plan · approve · reject · edit: <new message> · done MS-XXXXXX

**Can't:** send anything without approval, or act for anyone else.
