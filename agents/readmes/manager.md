# TeamReady Manager: the human approval gate

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

High-impact requests, such as production or analytics access, are prepared by TeamReady but only a human manager can approve them. This agent holds the request, shows the business reason and the verified evidence in a review card, and sends the decision back to the orchestrator, which tells the new hire.

**Managers can say:** pending · approve AR-XXXXXX · reject AR-XXXXXX <reason>
