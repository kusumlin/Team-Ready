# TeamReady Routing: who should I ask?

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Finds the best person to ask, with a score you can see: 50% skill match (including owning the relevant doc), 30% team match, 20% availability.

**Fairness rotation:** among people within 10% of the top score, the one with the lightest recent question load is chosen, so the same senior engineer isn't always the default.

**Learns:** 'wrong person' feedback lowers that person's score for that topic.

Never uses age, gender, race, nationality or other protected characteristics.

**Try:** Who should I ask about VPN problems?
